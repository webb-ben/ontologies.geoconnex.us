Title

Geoconnex alignment recommendations

Date Created

2026-06-02

Description

Recommended RDFS/OWL alignment axioms for an incomplete inventory of Geoconnex graph classes and properties. The ontology aligns hydrologic features with SawGraph/HY_Features, spatial descriptions with GeoSPARQL and Simple Features, web discovery metadata with Schema.org and DCAT, and observations/quantities with SOSA/SSN and QUDT.

http://purl.org/spatialai/spatial/water-full
